    Sound

    Use headphones. Space places each tone in its direction around the head; the centre always sounds inside the head. The tone code adds height as register (base low, crown high), depth as chord tone (back root, middle third, front fifth) and side as timbre (left soft, centre hollow, right bright).

    Map

    Rows run back to front, columns left to right, as you sit inside the head. Each layer sums to 126.

    Meta Quest

    Checking for a headset…

    Controls in VR. Trigger or pinch: point at a position to answer or inspect; pointing at empty space plays or pauses. Right stick: up makes the cube bigger, down smaller, left and right turn it around you. Left stick: slides the cube where you push it. Hold grip: grab the cube and move it; grip with both hands and pull apart or together to resize. B or Y: play or pause. A or X: reset size and place. With hand tracking, set the size here before you enter. In Head anchor the cube stays centred on your head, so only size changes.
